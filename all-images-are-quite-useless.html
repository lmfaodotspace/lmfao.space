All Images Are Quite Useless

xD SPACE presents All Images Are Quite Useless, a lecture performance by writer and artist @gideon___jacobs.

Inspired by Oscar Wilde’s declaration that “all art is quite useless,” Jacobs will examine this inflection point in the history of images through monologue, meditation, video, and AI.

This space will take place in English.
