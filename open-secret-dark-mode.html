Open Secret Dark Mode

xD SPACE is hosting Open Secret Dark Mode at La Générale on April 25, 2026. Doors open at 19:00, screening at 20:00. Open Secret Dark Mode is the second installation of Open Secret, a touring internet cinema consisting of a constantly transmuting series of screenings where networked culture, collective artistry and experimental filmmaking collide.

In this iteration, we've cut the lights. We're staring into the void. We confront the idea of the end and what it brings to the present and the future of intimacy and the image. Through play with artificial intelligence, found footage, and diary footage, the films approach catastrophe from multiple points of view, touching on cosmic narratives, ecological disaster, sexual desire, and death.

Dark Mode is composed of works by @driesmarchal, @cl0clo_o, @carcrashdaddy, @daemonlovers, @redactedcut, @linnphyllisseeger, @poorspigga, @nazovi.menya.sam, @jwyg, @dansdansrev, and @yveskleinlaurent.

85 minutes. No subtitles.
